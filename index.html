From hive to home

How FoRega honey moves, and where each step lives in this desk.

  1. 1
    Hives

    About 250 bee boxes, migrated between Gujarat and Kashmir. Workers inspect and feed the colonies.

  2. 2
    Harvest

    Honey is taken from our own boxes.

    Also bought from other beekeepersRecord the seller, kg and amount paid when you add stock.
    Stock → Add stock
  3. 3
    Lab test and batch

    Each lot gets a batch number and a lab report number, so any jar can be traced.

    Stock → Batch no. and Lab report no.
  4. 4
    Bottling

    Raw and unheated, packed in jars with the batch number, packing date and best-before on the label.

    Stock → jars in
  5. 5
    Order on WhatsApp

    Every buyer type has its own price.

    CustomerWholesalerOrder Partner
    Orders → New order
  6. 6
    Dispatch

    Jars come off stock automatically. Send the dispatch message and the bill.

    Orders → Mark dispatched · Bill
  7. 7
    Delivered

    Collect any balance, ask for a review and share it on WhatsApp Status.

    Orders → Mark delivered · Ads

Green steps happen at the hives. Navy steps happen on WhatsApp.

Backup and restore

All your data is saved on this phone only. Take a backup every week and send the file to your Google Drive or your own WhatsApp, so nothing is lost if the phone breaks or the app is removed.

Restoring replaces everything in the app with the backup file.
